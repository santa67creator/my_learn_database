# Decision Theory

Lecture notes for the course “Decision Theory” (15 lectures, optimization, multi-criteria choice, risk and uncertainty, game theory).

## Themes

**Fundamentals and mathematics**
- [[01 Basic Concepts of Decision Theory]] — decision maker, system analysis, decision-making strategies
- [[02 Measurement Theory and Modeling]] - sign/indicator/criterion, types of models
- [[03 Mathematical Model of a Problem Situation]] - ZPR, feasible and optimal solution
- [[04 Mathematical Tools for Decision-Making]] - efficiency criterion, guaranteed score, maximin

**Optimization**
- [[05 Static Optimization Problems]] — Weierstrass, Lagrange, linear and integer programming, simplex method
- [[06 Dynamic Optimization Problems]] - dynamic programming, Bellman equation, network planning

**Multi-criteria**
- [[07 Multi-Criteria Choice]] - binary relations, Pareto optimality
- [[08 Methods for Solving Multi-Criteria Problems]] - convolution of criteria, method of analyzing Saaty hierarchies

**Risk and uncertainty**
- [[09 Risk and Expected Utility]] - risk function, VaR, lotteries, von Neumann - Morgenstern
- [[10 Decision-Making Under Uncertainty]] - criteria of Wald, Savage, Hurwitz, Laplace

**Game Theory**
- [[11 Fundamentals of Game Theory]] - coalitions, Nash equilibrium, Pareto optimality, dominance
- [[12 Matrix and Non-Cooperative Games]] - saddle point, mixed strategies, von Neumann's theorem
- [[13 Cooperative Games]] — characteristic function, C-kernel, Shapley vector
- [[14 Dynamic Games]] - positional games, backward induction, perfect equilibrium
- [[15 Statistical Games]] - game with statistics with nature, Bayesian risk, Bayes formula
