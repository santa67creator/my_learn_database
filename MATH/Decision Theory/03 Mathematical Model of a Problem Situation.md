# Lecture 3. Mathematical model of a problem situation

- **Problem situation** - unsatisfactory state of the managed system from the point of view of the goals of the decision maker.
- **Decision making problem (DMP)** - search for an optimal management solution (DM) to transfer the system from an unsatisfactory state to a satisfactory one for the decision maker.
- **Admissible solution** - satisfies all the constraints of the problem (resource, time, logical).
- **Optimal solution** - transfers the system to an ideal (target) state.

## Stages of constructing a mathematical model
1. Conceptual model (approximate description of the situation).
2. Formalization (symbolic operations, restrictions, objective functions).
3. Checking for correctness and adequacy, simplification.

## Key concepts
- **Endogenous (internal)** variables - unknown characteristics of the model; **exogenous (external) / parameters** - known.
- **Closed model** - unambiguously determines internal quantities from external ones.
- **Tasks of analysis** (study of the properties of a given object) vs **tasks of synthesis** (selection of an object from a population).

## Model properties
**Productivity** (depends on the quality of the source data), **robustness** (resistance to errors in the source data), **visibility** (clear meaningful meaning of the components).

## Typical mathematical schemes (repeat from L.2)
Mathematical programming, game theory, differential equations, finite and probabilistic automata, queuing systems, Petri nets.
