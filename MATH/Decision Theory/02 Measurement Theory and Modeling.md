# Lecture 2. Theory of measurements. Modeling systems and processes

## Sign, indicator, criterion
- **Character** - an individual property of a unit of a population (qualitative or quantitative).
- **Indicator** is a generalized characteristic of a group of units or the entire population.
- **Criterion (optimality criterion)** - used to evaluate the effectiveness of solutions; must be specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound.

## Model and simulation
**Model** is a substitute object for the original, providing the study of its properties. Any model is subjective (reflects the researcher) and homomorphic (reflects only essential properties).

Requirements for the model: **adequacy**, **compliance with the problem being solved**, **compliance of the complexity of the model with the objectives of the study**, **block design**, **multivariance of elements**.

## Classification of models
| Sign | Types |
|---|---|
| Submission form | material (physical, analog) / ideal (intuitive, symbolic: logical, geometric, mathematical) |
| Completeness | complete / incomplete / approximate |
| Registration form | invariant / analytical / algorithmic |
| Purpose of modeling | simulation (analyzing) / optimization (synthesizing) |
| Linearity | linear (the principle of superposition is fulfilled) / nonlinear |
| Randomness | deterministic / probabilistic (stochastic) |
| Time | static / dynamic / quasi-static / stationary / quasi-stationary |

Typical mathematical schemes: **mathematical programming, game theory, differential equations, probabilistic automata, queuing systems, Petri nets**.

**Monte Carlo method** (method of statistical tests) is one of the main methods of simulation modeling: repeated reproduction of random processes with the same probabilistic characteristics as those of the system under study.
